# Future Fuel Airport

### Reimagining Airports for the Next Generation of Sustainable Aviation

Future Fuel Airport is a concept developed for the NASA Dream With Us Design Challenge: Fueling Future Flight.

The project explores how airports may evolve to support future aviation energy systems, sustainable operations, alternative fuels, and next-generation aircraft technologies.

---

## Vision

As aviation transitions toward new energy sources, airports must evolve alongside aircraft.

Future Fuel Airport investigates:

- Sustainable airport infrastructure
- Alternative aviation fuels
- Hydrogen and SAF integration
- Future aircraft operations
- Smart energy systems
- Environmental impact reduction
- Next-generation passenger experience

---

## Project Areas

- Aerospace Engineering
- Sustainable Aviation
- Airport Operations
- Alternative Fuels
- Energy Systems
- Flight Operations
- Systems Engineering
- Data Analysis
